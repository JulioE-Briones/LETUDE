01 · What we understood

One identity that many voices can carry

SWAC is an action-oriented nonprofit coalition of social work in Arkansas. A coalition's brand has to do more than look good:

Unite

Bring many members and partners together under one recognizable identity.

Be easy to use

Let members and partners apply it correctly, without a designer for every piece.

Include everyone

Stay readable and accessible for every person SWAC serves.

What SWAC asked for

A primary logo and variations, a brand guide (color, typography, accessibility), templates for social media, presentations, letterhead, email signature and business cards, and editable files. Optional: a visual refresh of the website, profile graphics and event applications.

03 · Concept prepared for SWAC

Route 1: "Many Hands, One Voice"

SWACSOCIAL WORK ACTION COALITION
Deep teal
Warm coral
Ink
Off-white

The idea: the coalition as a collective. Overlapping shapes, one per voice, come together and move forward as a single arrow.

  • Deep teal for trust, warm coral for care and action. Every text pair passes WCAG 2.1 AA.
  • Typography: an open-source sans serif for headlines and a humanist sans serif for body text.
  • Voice: direct, hopeful and plain-spoken. Working line: "Social work, in action."

Conceptual route prepared for this proposal. A second route is developed after discovery, and both are presented in week 2.
