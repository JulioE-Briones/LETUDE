02 · Proposed approach · Technical requirements

Open source, secure, and ready for AI-powered search

  • Open-source CMS: WordPress, set up so staff update news, facts, documents and pages without a developer.
  • Security and performance: HTTPS/TLS, firewall, malware scanning, image optimization and caching.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 with events for site views, downloads and inquiries.
  • Migration and redirects: current content reviewed and migrated, with redirects that preserve existing links and search visibility.
  • AI-powered search: schema.org structured data (organization, places, sites, FAQs, news), an entity-based content architecture, and short fact-dense summaries at the top of key pages, so AI tools can extract and cite the county's data accurately.
  • Training: a recorded session for designated staff, a written guide and full administrative access.
  • Launch and warranty: launch assistance and testing, then a 60-day post-launch warranty, twice the 30-day minimum.

08 · Tennessee/TVA listing integration

TVASites.com inside a branded experience, with a plan B

Primary approach

Embed the TVASites.com listings for Warren County inside a branded Sites & Buildings page, with our header, introduction, filters and contact call to action around it. In discovery we confirm with TVA which embed elements are available and how far they can be styled.

Limitations we plan for

Third-party embeds may not take our fonts and colors, may have their own accessibility and mobile-display limits, and depend on TVA's service. We test the embed on mobile and with assistive technology, and document anything we cannot fix.

Alternative

Native WordPress pages for featured sites and buildings: photos, acreage, utilities, map, distance to highways and a downloadable one-page PDF spec sheet, each linked to the full TVASites.com listing. Staff update them from the CMS.
