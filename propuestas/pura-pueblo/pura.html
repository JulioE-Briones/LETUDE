01 · Understanding PURA

Urban renewal is built in public. The website should show it.

PURA helps turn underused property in Pueblo into places people use, from the Riverwalk to downtown. Much of that work is visible only once a building opens. Before that, it lives in board packets, agreements and PDFs.

PURA already invites the community in: LAUNCH grants of up to $25,000, a set of development and service opportunities that includes a downtown hotel and a home on the Riverwalk, and an RFP for the Central Main Street parcels. The new website should make those invitations easy to find and answer.

Developers and investors

Open opportunities, site details, deadlines and how to submit.

Residents and taxpayers

What each project is, where it stands and what it delivers to Pueblo.

Small businesses

Grant programs, eligibility and application steps, in English and Spanish.

PURA staff and board

Agendas, minutes and reports published quickly, with a record the public can trust.

Audience priorities will be confirmed with PURA staff and site analytics during discovery.

Our working thesis

If people can see renewal, they can support it, apply to it and hold it accountable.

04 · The recommended solution

Four building blocks, one public face for renewal.

01

Project map and pages

An interactive map of PURA's urban renewal areas and projects. Each project gets a page with status, timeline, photos and documents.

02

Opportunities hub

Open RFPs, LAUNCH grants and development sites in one place, filtered by type, with deadlines and how to apply.

03

Public records center

Board agendas, minutes, budgets, plans and reports, searchable by year and type, posted by staff in a minute.

04

Accessible CMS, hosted and cared for

WordPress with simple forms for each content type, WCAG 2.1 AA templates, U.S. hosting and monthly care.

How an opportunity goes live
Fill in

Staff complete a form: title, type, deadline, summary, documents.

Check

The checker flags missing alt text or untagged PDFs.

Publish

The opportunity appears in the hub, on the map and on the homepage.

Close

On the deadline it moves to "closed" automatically, with results added later.

12 · The questions a careful committee should ask

We would ask them too. Here are our answers.

"Can a team outside the U.S. support us day to day?"

Argentina is 4 hours ahead of Mountain Time in winter, so our afternoon covers PURA's morning. Replies within one business day and live hours every weekday.

"What evidence do we have before we sign?"

The concepts in this proposal, a working session with your team and a clickable prototype in week 3. Payments follow approved milestones.

"Where is the site hosted?"

On managed hosting in U.S. data centers, with daily backups and security monitoring. Accounts are opened in PURA's name.

"What if one founder is unavailable?"

Both founders work on every project. Code lives in a shared repository, documentation is written as we go, and access is never held by one person.

"Can we leave if we want to?"

Yes. Open-source WordPress, no proprietary builder, care cancellable with 60 days' notice and a full handover package at no cost.

"Contracts, insurance and payments?"

Invoices in U.S. dollars, IRS Form W-8BEN-E as a foreign vendor, and certificates of insurance as PURA requires verify.

Our commitments

Fixed price. No change orders for anything in this scope.

Pay on approval. Three milestones PURA signs off.

30 days of stabilization after launch at no extra cost.

PURA's name on the domain, hosting and every account.
