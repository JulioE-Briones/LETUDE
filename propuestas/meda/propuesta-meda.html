01About TWO&HUMAN

TWO&HUMAN is a small, two-person web and digital marketing studio founded in 2026 by Julio Briones (web and digital marketing) and Mariam Neme (software engineering). We are based in Argentina and work remotely in English and Spanish. We are a new firm, and we say so plainly: we would rather earn MEDA's trust with a clear plan, honest pricing and full ownership for MEDA than with claims we cannot back up.

The RFP asks for a dependable partner, not the most elaborate website. That is how we work: simple sites that are fast, easy to update and fully owned by the client.

02Our understanding of MEDA's needs

MEDA needs a credible public home by February 2027: who MEDA is, what it does for Craig and Moffat County, how to reach it, and easy public access to meeting minutes, bylaws, founding documents and policies. It needs a dedicated email address, staff who can upload documents without calling a developer, low ongoing costs, and full control of the domain, site and content. And it needs a base that can grow into business-attraction pages and marketing in 2028, without starting over.

03Phase 1 approach

RequirementHow we will deliver it
PlatformWordPress, the most widely used website platform. It is open source with no license fees, and almost any web provider in Colorado or elsewhere can maintain it. We use a free, lightweight theme and free plugins only, so there are no paid licenses to transfer.
Landing pageOne clean, responsive page: MEDA's name and purpose, a short description of its mission and role in economic development, contact details, and a prominent "Meeting Minutes & Public Documents" section. Tested on desktop, tablet and mobile.
Document sectionA searchable document library organized by type (meeting minutes by year, bylaws, founding documents, policies and procedures), newest first. Staff upload a PDF, choose a category, and it appears on the site.
EmailA dedicated MEDA address (for example, info@ on MEDA's domain) on Google Workspace, set up with the records that keep email out of spam folders. Billed by Google directly to MEDA. Microsoft 365 is also possible if MEDA prefers.
DomainNo domain exists today, as Mr. Comstock confirmed. We will propose two or three short, available names and register the chosen one in MEDA's name. As a public corporation, MEDA may also be eligible for a free .gov domain; we can help apply if MEDA wishes (see optional features).
HostingManaged WordPress hosting on U.S. servers, with daily backups and a free security certificate (https). The account is in MEDA's name.
AccessibilityBuilt to WCAG 2.1 AA, the accessibility standard widely used for public-sector websites. Training covers how to publish accessible, searchable PDFs.
TrainingA recorded one-hour video session for the staff MEDA designates, plus a short written guide: uploading documents, editing text, and where to find each account.
