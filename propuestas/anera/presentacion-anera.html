01 · Our understanding

Anera.org is where people decide to support Anera's work

Donors, partners, journalists and job seekers learn about Anera's humanitarian and development work in the Middle East on anera.org, and many decide to give there. That puts four priorities at the top of the list:

Mobile

Fast and easy to use on a phone, where most visitors arrive.

Arabic, right to left

The Arabic version reads correctly with WPML on every template.

Accessibility

Usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technology.

Donations

The donation path must never break, in English or Arabic.

Anera also needs a partner who is reachable during East Coast business hours and keeps the site healthy all year, not only at launch.
