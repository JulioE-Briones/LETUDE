01About TWO&HUMAN

TWO&HUMAN is a two-person web and digital marketing studio founded in 2026 by Julio Briones (web, content and digital marketing) and Mariam Neme (software engineering: WordPress development, accessibility, integrations and support). We are based in Argentina, two hours ahead of Washington, DC, and we work in English and Spanish. We are a new firm, and we say so plainly: we would rather earn Anera's trust with a fixed-price first step, clear priorities and full ownership for Anera than with claims we cannot back up.

02Our understanding of Anera's needs

Anera.org is where donors, partners, journalists and job seekers learn about Anera's humanitarian and development work in the Middle East, and where many of them decide to give. That puts four things at the top of the list: the site must be fast and easy to use on a phone, the Arabic version must read correctly from right to left, the site must be accessible to everyone, and the donation path must never break. Anera also needs a partner who is reachable during East Coast business hours and who keeps the site healthy all year, not only at launch.

Our approach is to measure first and then fix what matters most, with Anera approving every fix and its cost before any work begins.

03Our approach

What Anera needsHow we will deliver it
Website auditA fixed-price review in four areas (technical health, mobile, Arabic/WPML and accessibility), with a written report and a one-hour review call. Delivered in 10 business days from access.
Mobile experienceSpeed testing with PageSpeed Insights and Core Web Vitals on key templates; layout, menu, forms and tap targets checked on real iOS and Android devices.
Arabic version (WPML)Right-to-left layout on every template, translation coverage, language switcher, menus, fonts and mixed Arabic/English content, checked page by page.
AccessibilityWCAG 2.1 AA review with automated tools (WAVE, axe) and manual checks: keyboard navigation, screen reader, color contrast, image descriptions and forms.

MaintenanceMonthly updates, security monitoring, backup checks and an hours bank for content and small improvements, with clear response times (section 07).
