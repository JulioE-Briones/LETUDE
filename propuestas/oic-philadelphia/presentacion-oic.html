01 · Mission and strategy 20%

Honor the legacy by making the next step obvious

Rev. Dr. Leon H. Sullivan founded OIC in Philadelphia in 1964 around a conviction he summed up in one line: "integration without preparation is frustration." Today the website is often the first door. It has to work as a service, not a brochure: a job seeker finds the right program and the next cohort in a few clicks, an employer knows how to hire or partner, and a donor understands why OIC matters now.

Next step first

Every audience gets its own clear door on the homepage. History supports the action; it does not stand in front of it.

Legacy as proof

A legacy band connects Rev. Dr. Sullivan's founding vision with what OIC participants achieve today, using outcomes OIC publishes.

One plain voice

Plain language at an easy reading level, with key pages available in Spanish as an option.

AsksAn inquiry form, or registration for an info session
Hears backThe right OIC team gets the inquiry; the person gets a confirmation

Inquiry routing

Each form goes to the right team (enrollment, employer relations or development), with an automatic confirmation to the person and a weekly export for OIC's records.

Research and task testing

Interviews with staff and a small group of participants, then task tests on the prototype, such as "find a program that starts next month and check if you qualify".

03 · Technical approach, accessibility and security 20%

Simple to run, accessible by default, owned by OIC

AreaHow we address it
PlatformWordPress with structured content types for programs and cohorts, an editor-friendly block layout and no proprietary page builder or lock-in.
AccessibilityWCAG 2.1 AA: contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader testing, accessible forms, text resizing and a public accessibility statement.
SecurityHardened configuration, a minimal set of maintained plugins, SSL, two-factor login, role-based access, automatic backups and updates.
PrivacyForms collect only what each team needs; no sensitive personal data is stored on the website beyond the inquiry itself.
AnalyticsPrivacy-conscious analytics with key events (program views, eligibility checks, inquiries, donate clicks) and a simple dashboard for staff and funders.
Migration and SEOContent inventory, URL map and redirects, so partners' links and search results keep working.
ControlOIC keeps the domain, hosting and every account. We work inside OIC-owned accounts from day one.
