01 · What we understood

A year of financing, made easy to read and easy to trust

OAQDA finances projects that improve Ohio's air quality and support its economy, through revenue bond financing for air quality facilities and grants that help small businesses invest in cleaner equipment. The annual report is how legislators, partners, businesses and the public see what was financed and what it achieved. It has four jobs:

Show the year

What OAQDA financed, where in Ohio and for whom, at a glance.

Prove the impact

Figures turned into simple charts, each with its source and year.

Tell real stories

Highlighted projects with a photo, a short story and a fact box.

Reach everyone

Readable in print, on a screen and with assistive technology.

The RFP asks for a report of about 32 pages, with print-ready, web-optimized and editable files, plus an accessible PDF if requested. We include the accessible PDF in the base price.
