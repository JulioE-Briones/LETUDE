01 · What we understood

Growing interest, a daily limit: growth has to be planned

Since 1895 the Incline has climbed Lookout Mountain at a grade of up to 72.7%, one of the steepest passenger railways in the world. It reopened in July 2025, and interest is growing: the RFP reports 77,741 website sessions in July 2026, up 34.2% year over year, with Atlanta as the main feeder market.

The August 2026 agreement with the Town of Lookout Mountain sets a limit of 2,000 riders per day, operating hours of 8:30 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., and stronger visitor messaging about the residential neighborhood.

What that means for marketing

More traffic on a busy Saturday adds little. The job is to fill the days and hours that have room, sell tickets before visitors arrive, and send every rider up the mountain knowing how to be a good neighbor.

In scope: paid search, paid social, display and native prospecting, SEO and monthly reporting for ridetheincline.com. Out of scope: website maintenance and organic social.
