01 · What we understood

Heritage and development, explained in a few clicks

The Central District Community Preservation and Development Authority was chartered by the Washington State Legislature in 2019. Its work, in its own words: to preserve the rich cultural heritage of the community, to mitigate the adverse effects of major public works and capital projects on the Central District, and to promote sustainable economic development.

That work is concrete: projects such as the McKinney Center and the revitalization of the historic Seattle Vocational Institute building, along with its community and business development work.

What the RFP asks for

  • A redesign of centraldistrictcpda.org.
  • A content audit before the new structure is built.
  • A usability update, so visitors find what they need quickly.
  • WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.

Our reading

The current site runs on WordPress with Elementor and Slider Revolution. Rebuilding it on native WordPress blocks makes it lighter, more accessible and easier for staff to update.

02 · From heavy to light

Same WordPress, without the weight

AreaWith Elementor and Slider RevolutionWith native WordPress blocks
SpeedPage builders and sliders add extra code and scripts to every page.Lean templates load faster, especially on phones and slower connections.
AccessibilityAuto-rotating sliders often fail WCAG 2.1 (success criterion 2.2.2, Pause, Stop, Hide) and are hard to use with a keyboard.Static, well-structured heroes and accessible components, tested with keyboard and screen readers.
EditingLayouts can break when staff change content; some edits need a developer.Locked, reusable patterns: staff change words and photos without breaking the design.
MaintenanceMore plugins mean more updates, more cost and more security risk.Fewer plugins to update, and standard WordPress any developer can maintain.

We confirm the details in the audit, page by page, before anything is rebuilt.
