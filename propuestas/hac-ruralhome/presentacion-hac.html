01 · What we understood

For many visitors, ruralhome.org is HAC's library

Since 1971, the Housing Assistance Council has supported affordable housing in rural America through lending, research, training and technical assistance, and policy work. Its website serves very different people: local housing organizations looking for loans and training, researchers and policymakers looking for data, journalists, and funders.

Local organizations

Find financing, training and technical assistance.

Researchers and policymakers

Find reports and data by topic, state and year.

Press

Get the key figure and the source, fast.

Funders and partners

See HAC's reach and impact at a glance.

The RFP describes the problems clearly: navigation that mirrors HAC's internal structure, accessibility gaps, weak mobile and performance, research locked in PDFs with limited search, and a site staff can't easily publish on.

02 · From problem to solution

Each problem in the RFP has a concrete answer

What the RFP describesWhat we do about it
Navigation mirrors internal structureNavigation built around visitor tasks: Lending, Research & Data, Training & TA, Policy, News and About, validated with 5 users before design.
Accessibility gapsWCAG 2.1 AA from the first template: semantic structure, contrast, keyboard navigation, alt text and accessible forms, tested automatically and by hand before launch.

Example rows showing the format. The real audit covers every page and publication on ruralhome.org.

How it works

  • Week 1–2: a full inventory with type, date, topic, traffic and a recommended action for each item.
  • HAC decides: staff confirm or change every recommendation.
  • Migration: all approved content moves to the new site, with metadata for the library.
  • Redirects: a 301 map for every changed address, tested before launch.
  • Yours to keep: the audit spreadsheet stays with HAC.
