01 · What we understood

Investors compare places fast; Alberta's case has to be clear

Invest Alberta works to attract investment to the province. This pre-qualification builds a roster of firms for ongoing or one-time marketing and communication work under a three-year master services agreement, with no guaranteed minimum spend. Proponents can apply for one or all service categories, from anywhere, as long as they meet the mandatory requirements.

For investment attraction, the audience is busy and global: executives, site selectors and partners deciding between many locations.
