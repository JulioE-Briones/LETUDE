01 · Understanding of NVHC's needs 20%

For many patients, the website is the first step to care

Nevada Health Centers is a Federally Qualified Health Center network serving communities across Nevada, from Las Vegas to Reno, Carson City and Elko, with family medicine, pediatrics, dental, behavioral health, pharmacy and more, plus mobile programs such as the Mammovan. It accepts Medicaid and Medicare and offers a sliding fee scale. Patients often arrive on a phone, often in Spanish, sometimes under stress. The site must answer four questions in seconds:

1

Where is the nearest center, and is it open?

Locations and mobile program stops, with hours, map and phone.
2

Who can see me, in my language?

Providers by specialty, location and language spoken.
3

Do you take my insurance, or can I pay less?

Medicaid, Medicare and the sliding fee scale, in plain words.
4

How do I get an appointment?

Call, request online or go to the patient portal, in one tap.

03 · Technical architecture, security and integrations 20%

Simple to run, secure by design, no patient data on the website

AreaHow we address it
PlatformWordPress with custom content types for locations, providers, services and mobile programs, editable by staff without code. No proprietary page builder.
IntegrationsLinks or embeds to NVHC's existing appointment-request and patient-portal systems, mapped in discovery. The finder can be fed by staff or by a scheduled import from NVHC's systems.
PrivacyThe website does not collect or store protected health information. Appointment and portal actions run in NVHC's existing systems; general contact forms ask for no health details.
SecurityHardened WordPress, SSL, web application firewall, least-privilege accounts, two-factor login, security monitoring, daily off-site backups and a tested restore.
AnalyticsGoogle Analytics 4 in NVHC's account, configured so no health information is sent to third parties and no tracking runs on appointment or portal pages. Conversion events and a monthly dashboard.
Migration and SEOContent inventory, URL map and 301 redirects, so patients and search engines still find every page.
BAAThe architecture avoids handling PHI. If NVHC determines a Business Associate Agreement is needed, TWO&HUMAN will sign one.
