01Our understanding of your needs

The Tecumseh BIA needs a partner who keeps its channels active every week and, above all, puts its member businesses in front of the community: two shoots a month, eight videos, ten posts and twenty Stories, coverage of mixers, night markets, grand openings and the AGM, SEO articles that link back to each featured business, routine website updates, the Tecumseh Dollars Sale, and a monthly meeting where results are reviewed and new ideas are brought to the table, not just tasks executed.

We read this as three goals: more people discovering Tecumseh businesses, more engagement and followers for the BIA, and more traffic to tecumsehbia.com and to the featured businesses. Everything below is built around those three goals.

05Approach & strategy

Growing the following

  • Collab posts with every featured business on Instagram, so each feature reaches the business's own followers, not only the BIA's.
  • A fixed weekly rhythm, so the community learns when to expect new content.
  • Re-shares of member and partner content, and local hashtags and keywords for Tecumseh and Essex County.

Increasing engagement

  • Polls, questions and "this or that" Stories every week.
  • Replies to comments within one business day; direct messages, complaints and sensitive matters forwarded to the BIA, as the RFP asks.
  • Daily interaction with member businesses, organizations and residents.

Promoting local businesses

  • Each featured business gets the full package: shoot, reel, carousel, Stories and an SEO article on tecumsehbia.com with a link back to its site.
  • Multi-business videos and carousels built around themes (date night, gifts, winter warm-ups), so several members benefit from one piece.

Compelling video content

  • Hook in the first two seconds, real people, real places, captions on every video, under 45 seconds for most reels.
  • Recurring series that people recognize and look forward to (see page 3).
  • The two review videos (6 and 12 months) show what the BIA achieved for its members.

Website traffic & SEO

  • Business features turned into SEO articles with clear titles, headings, metadata and backlinks to each business.
  • Every social post that has more to say points back to the website.
  • Monthly local SEO recommendations and routine updates to events, the directory, promotions and board information.

BIA initiatives & community awareness

  • Two initiative videos a month, a Tecumseh Dollars Sale campaign across the website and social channels, and full coverage of mixers, night markets, grand openings and the AGM.

Measuring and reporting

  • A baseline audit in December 2026, before the contract starts, so growth is measured from a real starting point.
  • A monthly report covering everything in section 11 of the RFP, reviewed at the strategy meeting with recommendations for the next month.

06How we would deliver from Argentina

On-site production in Tecumseh. The two monthly shoots and event coverage need someone on the ground, so we have kept that cost separate from our fee. The BIA can choose: (a) work with a local photographer and videographer of its choice, or (b) let us find one in the Windsor-Essex area. In both cases, TWO&HUMAN plans each shoot, prepares the shot lists, directs (live by video call when useful), edits, writes, publishes and reports. The photographer is paid at their own rate, with no markup from us. We estimate CAD 1,100 to 1,800 per month for two half-day shoots plus average event coverage. If you choose option (b), we will introduce the photographer before the contract starts.

Same working hours. Tucumán is two hours ahead of Tecumseh, so our working day fully overlaps with yours for meetings, approvals and quick replies.

Presentation. We would be glad to present to the Marketing Subcommittee by video call. If in-person attendance is required, please let us know and we will confirm our options.
