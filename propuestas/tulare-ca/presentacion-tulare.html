Understanding and approach

Fast, consistent work orders that keep the City's look

Economic Development needs a partner who can turn individual requests around quickly, such as business recruitment materials, promotional pieces and campaign content, while every piece looks and sounds like the City of Tulare. In a city where many residents and business owners speak Spanish, bilingual content is part of the job, not an extra.
