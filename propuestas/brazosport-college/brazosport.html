01 · Understanding Brazosport College

A once-in-a-generation moment to say who Brazosport is.

Brazosport College has served Lake Jackson and the Gulf Coast communities around it since 1968. Its students include recent graduates, working adults and people retraining for the region's employers, and its athletes now carry the Gators name.

The 60th anniversary gives the College a reason to tell its story with confidence. Doing the institutional brand, the athletics identity and the anniversary together is the chance to make them one family instead of three separate projects.

Prospective students

Should see a college that is close, practical and proud, and know where to start.

Students and athletes

Should want to wear the brand, on campus and on game day.

Employers and partners

Should recognize the College as a serious partner in the region's workforce.

Alumni and community

Should feel part of sixty years of Brazosport and want to celebrate it.

Audiences and priorities will be confirmed with the College through research in weeks 1 to 4.

Our working thesis

The strongest college brands are not invented by agencies. They are found in what students and staff already say, then made consistent.

12 · The questions a careful committee should ask

We would ask them too. Here are our answers.

"Can a studio outside Texas capture our community?"

Only by listening, which is why research comes first: interviews, student sessions and a survey with your own people. The brand will come from them, not from us.

"What evidence do we have before we sign?"

The concepts in this proposal, made for Brazosport before any contract. After award, the College approves the platform before design and chooses from three routes.

"Who owns the marks?"

The College. Copyright in all final artwork transfers on final payment, with every source file. Fonts and photos are licensed in the College's name.

"What about trademark risk?"

All marks are original. We run a preliminary search for conflicts before finalizing; registration, if desired, is handled by the College's counsel.

"What if one founder is unavailable?"

Both founders work on every project, files live in a shared library from day one and nothing depends on one person's computer.

"Contracts, insurance and payments?"

Invoices in U.S. dollars, IRS Form W-8BEN-E as a foreign vendor, and certificates of insurance as the College requires verify.

Our commitments

Fixed price. No change orders for anything in this scope.

Pay on approval. Three milestones the College signs off.

Research first. No design shown before the platform is approved.

Full ownership of every mark and source file.
