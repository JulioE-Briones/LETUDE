01 · What we understood

Two front doors for a 12-county region

Workforce Solutions of the Coastal Bend connects job seekers, youth and employers across the 12-county Coastal Bend region, from Corpus Christi to its rural communities, with jobs, training, child care support and hiring services. Many of the people you serve read in Spanish, use a phone as their only screen, or rely on assistive technology. The website has four jobs:

Job seekers

Find a job, get training or child care support, in a few taps.

Employers

Post jobs, recruit, train staff and join hiring events.

Everyone, in their language

The same information in English and Spanish, on every page.

Accessible by law

Usable by people with disabilities, as federal workforce programs require.

RFP 26-01 asks for website design and web application development that meet DOL accessibility requirements, Section 508 and WCAG 2.1 AA, aimed at employers and job seekers. That accessibility-first scope is how we already work.

05 · Accessibility and compliance

Accessible from the first sketch, not fixed at the end

Standards we build to

  • WCAG 2.1 AA on every template and component.
  • Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act.
  • WIOA Section 188 and 29 CFR Part 38: equal access for people with disabilities and people with limited English proficiency.
  • Equal opportunity notice with Relay Texas 711 on every page.

How we test it

  • Automated checks on every template during development.
  • Manual keyboard-only and screen reader testing (NVDA and VoiceOver) before launch.
  • Color contrast and text resizing checks up to 200%.
  • Accessible forms with clear labels and error messages, in both languages.
  • A written accessibility report and a public accessibility statement at launch.
