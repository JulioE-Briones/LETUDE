01 · What we understood

A new profession needs a brand people want to earn

The DPPC is a new, industry-built credential to professionalize damage prevention. Utility Safety Partners underwrites it, guided by its Training Standards Committee and an Advisory Council. A CORE certification applies to every role, course standards follow the competencies of a damage prevention professional, and continuing education credits keep the certification current.

The program is global, and the RFP issued on November 2 asks for a brand with working templates, such as social media and email newsletters.

04 · A route prepared for the DPPC

Certified to know the line.Headlines and text in a sturdy, open sans-serif such as Inter or Source Sans 3: free, multilingual and legible at small sizes.

The idea

Every damage prevention practice comes down to one moment: knowing exactly where the line is before you dig. The mark is one continuous line that runs underground, as a dotted locate line, and surfaces as a check mark. It joins "locate" and "verified".

Why it works

  • Field-tough: one bold shape that reads on a hard hat or a truck.
  • Credible: navy leads; orange is the signal, not the noise.
  • A nod to the trade: secondary colors echo the marking colors crews use in North America, without depending on them abroad.

Concept prepared for this proposal. It is a starting point for discussion, not a final design, and has not been tested with USP stakeholders.

04 · Three routes, one decision

Three real alternatives, chosen in week 3

ROUTE A

The Safe Line

The locate line that surfaces as a check mark. Practical and field-first, as shown on the previous slides.

ROUTE B

Ground Truth

Contour lines and soil layers drawn as a precise, technical pattern. Calm and expert; strongest with regulators and engineers.

ROUTE C

The Professional Seal

A modern seal that borrows the confidence of established professional designations. Formal and instantly read as a credential.
