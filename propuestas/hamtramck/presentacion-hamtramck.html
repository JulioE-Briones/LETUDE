01 · What we understood

Green space and recreation for every neighbor

The Hamtramck Parks Conservancy brings together the City, Hamtramck Public Schools and community partners to operate, improve and provide equitable access to green spaces and recreation in Hamtramck, including Veterans Memorial Park and Historic Hamtramck Stadium. Its website has four clear jobs:

Find a park

Where each park is, what it offers and what is happening there this week.

Get involved

Volunteer at a stewardship day, donate or partner, in one click.

Show the impact

Projects, annual reports and updates for funders and the City.

Open to everyone

Readable on any phone and by everyone, in a city where many languages are spoken.

The current site has the basics, but some pages still need care (the About page shows placeholder text). The redesign gives the Conservancy a site its own team can update without a developer.
