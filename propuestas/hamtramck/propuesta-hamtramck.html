We are based in Argentina, two hours ahead of Michigan in winter, and work remotely in English and Spanish. 02Our understanding of the Conservancy's needs

The Hamtramck Parks Conservancy brings together the City, Hamtramck Public Schools and community partners to operate, improve and provide equitable access to green spaces and recreation in Hamtramck, including Veterans Memorial Park and Historic Hamtramck Stadium. Its website should make that work visible and easy to join: where the parks are and what each one offers, what is happening this month, how to volunteer at a stewardship day, and how to donate or partner.

The current site has the basics, but some pages still need care (the About page shows placeholder text). The redesign is the chance to put the most important actions, find a park, see events, volunteer and donate, one click from the home page, on a site that staff and volunteers can update without a developer. And since many languages are spoken in Hamtramck, key pages can be offered in more than one.

03Our approach

AreaHow we will deliver it
PlatformWordPress: open source, no license fees, and easy for staff and volunteers to update. If the Conservancy prefers to stay on its current platform, we can work within it; we confirm this after reviewing the current setup.
Current contentWe review every current page, keep what is up to date, remove placeholder text and lightly edit for clarity. The Conservancy approves all text.
Parks directoryOne page per park, with a map, amenities, rules and hours, photos and current projects, starting with Veterans Memorial Park and Historic Hamtramck Stadium.
Events and volunteeringAn events calendar with stewardship days and community events, linked to the Eventbrite registration the Conservancy already uses, plus a volunteer sign-up form.
Support the parksA visible Donate button connected to the Conservancy's current donation platform, a partners and sponsors section, a page for annual reports, and newsletter sign-up.
Languages (optional)Key pages in additional languages chosen by the Conservancy (for example Arabic, Bengali and Polish), with a language switcher. Translations are provided or reviewed by the Conservancy's community partners.
AccessibilityBuilt to WCAG 2.1 AA: readable on phones, keyboard navigation, image descriptions and good color contrast.
TrainingA recorded one-hour video session and a short written guide: adding events, updating park pages and uploading reports.
