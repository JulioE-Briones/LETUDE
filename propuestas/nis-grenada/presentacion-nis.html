01 · What we understood

A brand that carries people's working lives

Since the National Insurance Act of 1983, the National Insurance Scheme has been Grenada's social security institution for Grenada, Carriacou and Petite Martinique. It collects contributions and pays benefits for sickness, maternity, employment injury, invalidity, age, survivors and funerals, and it also offers loans.

People trust the NIS with their working life's contributions. A new brand must reassure them, not confuse them, and make it easier to understand how to claim what they are owed.

03 · Concept prepared for NIS Grenada

Route A: "Steady Hands"

NISNATIONAL INSURANCE SCHEME · GRENADA
Sea blue
Nutmeg
Growth green
Warm neutral

The idea: the NIS as the steady hand that supports Grenadians from their first job to retirement. A sheltering arc rises from the horizon line, suggesting security, the sea and the land.

  • Sea blue for stability, nutmeg as a respectful nod to the Spice Isle (validated in research), green for growth.
  • Typography: a sturdy open-source sans serif, readable on forms and in small print.
  • Working line: "Here for every stage of your working life."

Conceptual route prepared for this proposal. A second route is developed after research, and both are presented in week 4.
