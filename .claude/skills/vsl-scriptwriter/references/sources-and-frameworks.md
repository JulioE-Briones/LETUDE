## Select a structure

- A is the default for focused product or service explanations.
- B suits a longer narrative when justified by audience and offer complexity.
- C's factual questions suit familiar services where friction concerns scope, availability, process, or trust.
- Combine only functions the buyer needs. Do not stack three complete frameworks.
