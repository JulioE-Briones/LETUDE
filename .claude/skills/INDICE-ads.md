# Proyecto 02: Ads (media buyer)

Fuente (licencia MIT): github.com/zubair-trabzada/ai-ads-claude

Cada .zip es una skill. Súbelas en Configuración → Capacidades → Skills.

## Copy y Creativos
- **ads-copy**: Generates 10+ ad copy variations for any major ad platform with A/B testing, character-count compliance, and four proven copy frameworks (PAS, AIDA, BAB, 4Ps)
- **ads-hooks**: Generates 20+ scroll-stopping ad hooks organized by psychological angle (pain, curiosity, social proof, contrarian, urgency) with platform-specific formatting, 
- **ads-creative**: Creative Brief Generator for designers, video editors, and content teams
- **ads-video**: Generates complete video ad scripts for 15s, 30s, and 60s formats with shot-by-shot breakdowns, voiceover scripts, on-screen text, B-roll suggestions, music dir

## Audiencia y Embudo
- **ads-audience**: Builds 5-7 detailed audience personas from a URL with demographics, psychographics, pain points, buying triggers, platform-specific targeting parameters, person
- **ads-funnel**: Full Ads Funnel Architect. Builds a complete advertising funnel across TOFU, MOFU, BOFU, and Retargeting stages with platform-specific templates, audience targe
- **ads-landing**: Landing Page Audit & Optimizer. Analyzes landing pages for ad-to-page alignment, message match, CTA clarity, trust signals, mobile optimization, and form fricti

## Testing y Presupuesto
- **ads-testing**: A/B Testing Plan Generator. Creates structured testing roadmaps with prioritized test sequences, duration calculators, sample size requirements, statistical sig
- **ads-budget**: Budget Allocation & ROI Projector. Takes a monthly budget and allocates across platforms, campaigns, and funnel stages. Includes platform-specific CPM/CPC/CPA e

## Auditoria y Competencia
- **ads-audit**: Ad Performance Auditor — analyze existing ad campaigns and identify optimization opportunities
- **ads-quick**: 60-Second Ad Readiness Snapshot — quick assessment without subagents
- **ads-competitors**: Competitive Ad Intelligence. Analyzes competitor advertising strategies including platform identification, landing page analysis, targeting reverse-engineering,
